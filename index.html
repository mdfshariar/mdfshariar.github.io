01About

I work at the bench with bacteria and at the computer with mass spectrometry data.

I hold a BSc in Biochemistry and Molecular Biology from Jagannath University. At the Food, Nutrition and Agriculture Research Laboratory, CARS, University of Dhaka, I isolate and profile resistant bacteria from food, water, environmental and clinical samples. At the Division of Mass Spectrometry and Proteomics, The Red-Green Research Centre, Dhaka, I analyse LC-MS/MS based proteomics and lipidomics data.

My long-term aim is to build a national genomic AMR database in Bangladesh that links human, food and environmental isolates, and to grow local bioinformatics capacity.
